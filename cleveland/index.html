Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Cleveland

Routine maintenance makes all the difference between a system that lasts and one that breaks down in the middle of a heat wave. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a new filter every one to three months, two feet of clearance around the outdoor condenser, a spring rinse of the condenser coil, and two professional tune-ups a year, so small issues get caught early.

Equipment age plays a big role. With regular service, a central air conditioner usually runs 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts showing warning signs, like higher energy bills, rooms that won't reach the setpoint, or repeat service calls, it's smart to start planning a replacement. Replacing on your own schedule is cheaper and is far less stressful than an emergency swap during the first heat wave of the year.

The sooner you call about a comfort problem, the smaller the repair tends to be. A capacitor that's starting to fail is an easy, low-cost part to replace, yet that same capacitor, neglected through a full season, can ruin the compressor and turn a simple fix into a full system replacement. Typical Heating & Cooling Issues in Cleveland

Most local HVAC failures give warning signs before they happen.
